With several rooms on one key, each press sets them all to the same level: the quietest one plus or minus the step. Rooms ticked under Keep own level are not equalized; they keep their own level and move by the step along with the others. The key shows the level now and the level a press lands on, e.g. 10→12. On a Stream Deck + dial: turn for volume, press for mute. Only needed when no speakers show up above (multicast discovery blocked): the IP of any one Sonos speaker.
