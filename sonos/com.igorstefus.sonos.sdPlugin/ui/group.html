Group joins the ticked rooms into one group that plays what the lead speaker is playing. Ungroup makes each ticked room standalone. Key shows green while they are grouped. With a single room ticked, the toggle takes it out of its group, and puts it back into the lead speaker's group (or the group it left, or the one that is playing). Only needed when no speakers show up above (multicast discovery blocked): the IP of any one Sonos speaker.
